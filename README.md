# CRM_analytics_EXCEL
## FIRST SCOPE

### Story Overview
The goal of this project was to explore e-commerce sales data from a CRM perspective, using Excel to reveal patterns in customer behavior and the impact of advertising. Starting from basic data quality checks, I gradually uncovered key business insights: a high customer churn rate, profitable LTV/CAC ratios, and regression coefficients that guide advertising budgets. Each step builds on the previous one, transforming raw spreadsheets into an actionable business story.
________________________________________
Data
The dataset contains 581,587 rows and 8 columns: Invoice, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, Price, CustomerID, Country.
This raw sales and customer data forms the foundation for the entire analysis.
________________________________________
1.	Initial Exploration — Missing and Filled Cells

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8101f7e-4a90-450b-bf8a-3cff6987898e)

To evaluate data quality, I counted blank, numeric, and filled cells using COUNTBLANK, COUNTA, and COUNT. This revealed where cleaning was needed and highlighted columns with potential business impact if left incomplete.
2.	Basic Statistics — Mean and Standard Deviation

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2b3326db-ceda-4fd6-a6b1-aa7ceb14d179)

I calculated averages with AVERAGE and standard deviations with STD to understand sales and quantity distributions. These metrics set the baseline for identifying unusual values and understanding typical sales behavior.
3.	Invoice and Customer Summaries

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/45843885-45db-4af6-b9c6-951e93c12688)

Using unique Invoice IDs and CustomerID, I measured basket counts, total customers, and average basket amounts. These summaries provided a clear picture of sales volume and customer engagement.
4.	Finding the Top-Selling Product

With INDEX and MATCH, I identified the product with the highest sales, offering guidance for inventory and promotion priorities.
5.	Correlation Between Ad Channels and Sales

By aligning product sales data with advertising channels, I calculated correlations and reviewed missing data, averages, medians, and standard deviations to assess each channel’s contribution to revenue.
6.	Outlier Analysis

<img width="1213" height="499" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f81f59c3-a8d4-4aa2-8de4-d7b5e52b47ed" />

Splitting time periods into quarters, I defined upper and lower limits to detect seasonal effects, campaign spikes, or data errors that produced extreme sales values.

<img width="527" height="247" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/22787182-e625-4b27-a344-497b9f2d7bde" />
